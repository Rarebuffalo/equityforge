Pondy Oxides and Chemicals Limited (POCL) — Q2FY26 Result Update
Sector: Chemicals & Metals
Report Date: 28 October 2025
Rating: BUY | Target Price: Rs. 1,450 | CMP: Rs. 1,120

Executive Summary:
Pondy Oxides and Chemicals Limited (POCL) reported strong Q2FY26 financial results driven by increased demand for recycled lead and non-ferrous alloys. Revenue grew by 32.4% YoY to Rs. 485.2 Cr compared to Rs. 366.5 Cr in Q2FY25.

Key Financial Highlights:
- Q2FY26 Revenue from Operations: Rs. 485.2 Cr (YoY +32.4%, QoQ +14.2%)
- Q2FY26 EBITDA: Rs. 42.8 Cr (EBITDA Margin: 8.8%, up 120 bps YoY)
- Q2FY26 Net Profit (PAT): Rs. 26.5 Cr (YoY +41.2%)
- EPS for Q2FY26: Rs. 22.8 per share
- 6-Month Average Volume: 0.15 Cr shares
- Free Float: 48.5%
- Promoters Holding: 51.5%

Business Overview & Investment Thesis:
POCL is a leading secondary lead smelter and chemical manufacturer in India. The company continues to benefit from stricter environmental regulations encouraging circular economy practices and lead recycling. Expansion of capacity at Chittoor plant remains on track for completion by Q4FY26.

Financial Estimates:
FY24A Sales: Rs. 1,420 Cr | EBITDA: Rs. 110 Cr | PAT: Rs. 64 Cr
FY25A Sales: Rs. 1,780 Cr | EBITDA: Rs. 145 Cr | PAT: Rs. 88 Cr
FY26E Sales: Rs. 2,150 Cr | EBITDA: Rs. 185 Cr | PAT: Rs. 118 Cr

Key Risks:
1. Fluctuations in international lead LME prices.
2. Changes in environmental regulations and scrap import quotas.
3. Supply chain disruptions in raw material sourcing.
